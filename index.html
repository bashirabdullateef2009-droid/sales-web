How we test

AyoTech exists because a spec sheet can be true and still be misleading. A "120Hz" panel that only holds 120Hz on a cooled lab bench isn't 120Hz on your desk in a warm room. We buy the same laptops, monitors, gaming systems and TVs anyone can buy, run them under the load and hours you'd actually put them through, and sell the ones that hold up against their own claims.

Every listing on this page carries the number we measured, not the number printed on the box. Where they match, that's the number you see. Where they don't, we either adjust the listing to say so or we don't carry the model.

We cover four categories — laptops, monitors, gaming systems and TVs — because that's where the gap between marketing and daily use tends to be widest. We'd rather be right about four things than vague about forty.
